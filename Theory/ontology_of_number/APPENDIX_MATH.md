# Appendix A: Key group‑theoretic facts

## A.1 E8 root system

- 240 roots = 112 (D8 type) + 128 (spinor type)
- D8 type: two coordinates `±2`, others 0.
- Spinor type: all coordinates `±1`, even parity (product of all signs = +1).
- Dot product spectrum against any fixed root:

  ```
  {-8:1, -4:56, 0:126, +4:56, +8:1}
  ```

- `126` = number of orthogonal roots; equals `dim(E7) - rank(E7)`.

## A.2 Branching chain

- `E8 ⊃ E6 × SU(3)`; `E6 ⊃ SO(10) × U(1)`; `SO(10) ⊃ SU(5) × U(1)`
- The fundamental representation `27` of E6 breaks as `16 + 10 + 1` under SO(10).
- This yields the Standard Model fermions (16‑plet) plus Higgses (10‑plet) and singlets.

## A.3 Mass formula

- The exponential mass formula `m = m_e · exp( (3/2) · n / 2 )` arises from the **geodesic distance** in the Cartan subalgebra from the electron (n=0) to the fermion's Cartan weight. The factor `3/2` is the ratio `N_gen / rank(G₂)` and is fixed by three independent routes (Casimir of SU(2)_L, rank ratio, spin‑1 Dirac shift).

## A.4 Cosmological constant

- The formula `Λ = 4 · (1/9)^128` follows from the **fractal penetration depth** of the ternary axis in the Cube27 structure, with depth equal to the number of spinor roots (128). The factor 4 is the tick period squared from the halving dynamics.

All these are independent of any cosmological or particle‑physics data.
